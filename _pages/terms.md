## Privacy Policy

This website is a personal portfolio. It does not collect contact form submissions, account information, or comments.

### Display Preferences

When you choose a light or dark theme, this site stores that preference in your browser's local storage. It is used only to remember the display setting.

### Analytics

This site may use Google Analytics and PostHog to understand aggregate traffic patterns, such as page views, referrers, device types, and approximate regions. These services may use cookies or similar technologies. The data is used only to improve the site and is not sold by me.

You can learn more in the [Google Analytics Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
You can also learn more in the [PostHog Privacy Policy](https://posthog.com/privacy).

### External Links

Some pages link to third-party services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, Medium, Instagram, OpenReview, arXiv, ACM, IEEE, and Google Drive. Those services have their own privacy practices.
